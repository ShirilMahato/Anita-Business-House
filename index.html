Introduction

Anita Business House Pvt. Ltd. is a leading distribution and service provider based in Kathmandu, Nepal. With over two decades of experience, our company has established a reputation for reliability, trustworthiness, and supply chain excellence. With a proud history dating back to 2003, our company has established a reputation for reliability, trustworthiness, and supply chain excellence. We specialize in the distribution of a diverse range of high-quality products, building strong partnerships with suppliers, and serving customers across Nepal.

Mission

To emerge as the foremost distribution and service provider in Nepal, known for our unwavering commitment to being a reliable supplier of quality products.

Vision

To emerge as Nepal’s most trusted and preferred distribution and service partner, setting the industry benchmark for excellence, innovation, and nationwide reach.
