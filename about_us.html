About Us

Welcome to Anita Business House Pvt. Ltd., based in Kathmandu, Nepal, where over two decades of experience have forged a legacy of reliability, trustworthiness, and supply chain excellence. Since our establishment in 2003, we've been dedicated to delivering high-quality products and cultivating strong partnerships with suppliers, all while serving customers throughout Nepal. Our unwavering commitment to quality and customer satisfaction fuels our journey, making us a trusted partner on your path to success.
At Anita Business House Pvt. Ltd., our story is one of growth, excellence, and enduring dedication. We take pride in our history and look forward to continuing our mission of providing top-tier products and services, as we remain your steadfast ally in business.

Mission

To emerge as the foremost distribution and service provider in Nepal, known for our unwavering commitment to being a reliable supplier of quality products.
We aim to nurture a trustworthy brand image, along with transparent and crystal-clear management practices.

Our Focus

Our focus on cultivating harmonious networking and relationships is the fundamental cornerstone of our operation.
We work to strive to optimize supply chain efficiency to not only meet but exceed our customer expectations while fostering sustained growth and market leadership.

Objective

To provide superior and timely services to our clients ensuring their satisfaction and loyalty and further expanding our extensive distribution network.

Vision

To emerge as Nepal’s most trusted and preferred distribution and service partner, setting the industry benchmark for excellence, innovation, and nationwide reach.

Core Value

To emerge as Nepal’s most trusted and preferred distribution and service partner, setting the industry benchmark for excellence, innovation, and nationwide reach.

Quality Policy

We are dedicated to delivering high-quality products and services, adhering to rigorous quality standard at every stage as our quality policy encompass product inspection, testing, and compliance with industry regulation.
