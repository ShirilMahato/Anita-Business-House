Stanfresh

Stanvac Prime, a subsidiary of Stanvac Chemicals India, offers high-quality aerosol and non-aerosol solutions, collaborating with WhaleSpray, Spain, since 2018. They cover personal care, household, OTC drugs, and industrial solutions with brands like Adigo, Inflame, and Stanrelief. Products include grooming items, household cleaners, and OTC essentials. Their manufacturing facilities are situated in Gurugram, Haryana, and Mayapuri, Delhi, India. Currently supported by 300 distributors, Anita business house PVT. LTD. is their primary distributor of Nepal.
